From a first colored line to everything a line can carry, one short chapter at a time. Read the
pages in order:

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md) - install, write a first line, color its segments.
2. [Colors](colors.md) - names, hex codes, `rgb()` and `hsl()`, and the 256 and 16 colors.
3. [Text styles](styles.md) - bold, italic, underline styles and colors, reverse and the rest.
4. [Gradients](gradients.md) - blend colors across the text and its background.
5. [Markup and highlighting](markup.md) - color part of a string with tags, patterns and splits.
6. [Layout](layout.md) - padding and alignment, wrapping and cutting, indentation and blank lines.
7. [Style profiles](profiles.md) - the message helpers, reusable styles and the default style.
8. [Logging](logging.md) - write each line to a file as well, with a time and a level.
